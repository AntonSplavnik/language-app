# Streak

Right answers in a row towards learned (`LEARNED_STREAK`, 3), in two sizes:

- **Dots** at the end of a My words row: three 8 px dots, gap 4, filled `accent-ink` up to the streak, `track` otherwise. A word never practised shows the NEW pill instead; learned and ignored words show nothing.
- **Segments** on Word detail's progress card: one 10 px segment per answer across the card (gap 6) under "Streak … 1/3".
- Always add the spoken text ("1 of 3 right in a row") as screen-reader text.
