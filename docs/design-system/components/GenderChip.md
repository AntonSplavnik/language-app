# GenderChip

The noun's gender as a small uppercase chip over the word on Word detail: "DER · MASCULINE", "DIE · FEMININE", "DAS · NEUTER".

- 13/700, letter-spacing 0.06em, padding 5 px 10 px, fully round; gender `-soft` ground with the gender colour as text (at least 5.3:1 in both themes).
- Nouns with an article only; one per screen.
