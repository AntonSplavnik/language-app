# DropZone

The first step of an import, before any photo: one big target for the camera or the photo library.

- Min 220 px, padding 28, `radius-hero`, 1.5 px dashed `track-strong`, no fill (pressed `surface`).
- A 72 px `accent` square (radius 24) with the camera icon, then "Take or choose photos" in display 22/800 and "Photograph the pages you studied" in 14 px `muted`.
- Once photos exist it becomes the **Add more photos** cell at the end of the photo grid (see PhotoTile).
