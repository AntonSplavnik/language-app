# SearchField

The search on My words: 48 px, `radius-field`, `surface` + `border`, a 18 px search icon inside at the left in `muted`, 16 px text.

- Placeholder "Search German or English"; `aria-label` "Search your words"; `type="search"`, `enterKeyHint="search"`, no autocorrect.
- Focus: 2 px `accent-ink` outline inset by 1 px. Searches 250 ms after typing stops, or at once on Enter.
