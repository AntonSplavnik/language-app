# BigAction

Home's main action: a full-width lime block (`radius-hero`, min 120 px) with a big label, a second line and a dark circle holding the play icon.

- Label in `label-big` (32/800), second line 15/600 at 75% opacity, circle 60 px in `on-accent` with a lime play icon.
- **Practice** when there are learning or learned words (second line: "149 words to practise" / "Review learned words"); before there are words, **New import** takes this place (plus icon, no second line) with the hint under it.
- It is the only lime on Home besides the deck bar's ink. Pressed: `accent` 90% with `on-accent`.
- Consumer provides: the label, the optional second line, the icon, the link.
