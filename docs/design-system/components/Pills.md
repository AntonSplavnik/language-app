# Pills

Small fully-round labels that sit on a row or card. Pick by meaning:

- **NEW** (`badge`): a learning word never practised; `accent-soft` ground, `accent-ink` text.
- **Part of speech** on the card front: 13/600, `surface-raised` + `muted`.
- **not in dictionary** under a Review headword: 12/600, `surface-raised` + `muted`.
- **Details pending** on the card back: `warning-soft` + `warning`.
- **Counts** in tabs and the Review words button: 12–13/700, tabular numerals.
- Text only, lower case except NEW (uppercase via CSS); never an icon-only pill.
